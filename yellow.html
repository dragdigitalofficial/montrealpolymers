OUR CORE ENVIRONMENTAL DIFFERENCE

Why We Never Use Chemical Machines or Acid Washes.

Many recycling companies rely on aggressive chemical baths, chlorinated solvents, and high-heat decomposition that degrade polymer chains and discharge toxic wastewater into Canadian waterways.

Montreal Polymers pioneered a 100% cold mechanical circular process. We use heavy rotary granulators, high-gauss neodymium magnetic separators, and air-density gravity tables. The result is pure, re-usable polymer regrind that saves thousands of tons from landfills without creating a single drop of hazardous chemical byproduct.

Zero Water Runoff

Clean, dry physical separation prevents soil and municipal water contamination.

Preserved Intrinsic Viscosity

Cold processing preserves the virgin mechanical strength of PVC & HDPE polymers.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

How Montreal Polymers Processes Industrial Scrap

A disciplined 4-stage mechanical protocol that safeguards purity, preserves polymer chains, and eliminates landfill waste.

01

Dock Receiving & Sort

Scrap arrives via drop trailer or flatbed. Every lot is weighed on certified scales and inspected for polymer grade, color segregation, and purity.

Certified Scale Ticket Issued
02

Magnetic De-Ironing

Heavy cross-belt neodymium magnets extract screws, steel fasteners, and metallic inserts from window frames or assembly trimmings.

12,000 Gauss Neodymium
03

Cold Mechanical Granulation

Heavy-duty water-cooled rotor knives reduce rigid pieces into uniform 3/8" regrind flakes without thermal burn or chemical solvents.

0% Chemical Solvents Used
04

Aspiration, QA & Dispatch

Air classifiers remove micro-fines and dust. Flakes are packed into lined gaylord boxes or bulk bags, barcoded, and lab-certified for dispatch.
