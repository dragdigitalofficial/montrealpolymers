About Montreal Polymers

Why We Never Use Chemical Washing Machines or Solvents

Traditional plastic recyclers rely on heavy chemical solvent baths, caustic acid dissolving agents, or high-temperature incinerators. This degrades the polymer chains, emits dangerous chlorine fumes, and leaves toxic slurry that contaminates waterways and soil.

At Montreal Polymers, we operate on a strictly Cold Mechanical Process: precision manual and optical sorting, ambient-temperature rotary granulators, and acoustic cyclonic dust extraction. No chemicals touch our polymers.

How We Operate

4-Step Cold Mechanical Workflow
